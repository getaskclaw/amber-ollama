## Findings

1. **Model name ≠ capability, now proven on a full library.** ollama's glm-5.3-flash passes 3 cases its crof namesake fails, including the suite's hardest build case (7/7 vs 1/7) and the vision case (3.0 vs -2). Vendor routing, quantization, or serving stack differences are material. Benchmark the endpoint, not the name.
2. **The vision case is falling.** A-ea80d793 was first passed by our frontier anchor (2.0, 2026-09-05); ollama's glm-5.3-flash now holds the best score (3.0) — the only non-frontier-priced model that can do visual review at all. All five crof serves failed it.
3. **deepseek-v4-flash:0731: same total, different temper.** Both vendors land at 13/21, but crof's adversarial-review run fabricated 12 findings (-12) while ollama's stayed nearly clean (-2); ollama's instead lost one ops case. For review duty, ollama's is the safer serve of the same name.
4. **Both models die on the same walls.** A-d511f9e8 (4/12, again identical), A-cdc3d11a (nobody passes adversarial review anywhere yet). These cases continue to mark the suite's frontier.
5. **Near-miss at the top:** both models scored 14/15 on A-a317e74b — the case only qwen3.8-27b (crof) has ever maxed (15/15, same day).
